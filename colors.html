Color System & Usage

The CanJet brand uses a bold red triad palette representing energy, passion, and excitement. These colors define buttons, highlights, semantic states, and UI structure.

Brand

Primary
--color-primary: #ED1C24

Usage: primary actions, headers

Secondary
--color-secondary: #F54748

Usage: accents, secondary states

Accent
--color-accent: #F56F6C

Usage: highlights, info panels

Semantic

Success
--color-success: #22C55E

Positive trend or success state

Warning
--color-warning: #F59E0B

Caution or pending attention

Danger
--color-danger: #EF4444

Error or negative trend

Neutrals

Surface
--color-surface: #FFFFFF

Cards, containers

Background
--color-background: #F9FAF9

Page background

Text
--color-text: #1E293B

Text color
