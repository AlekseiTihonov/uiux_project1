Button Styles

Buttons start actions in the dashboard. Use a primary button for the main action on a screen, and secondary or disabled buttons for supporting actions.

Primary Button

The primary button is used for the most important action on a page, such as “Save” or “Apply”. 

<button class="btn btn-primary">Primary Button</button>

Secondary Button

The secondary button is used for less important actions that are still available to the user. 

<button class="btn btn-secondary">Secondary Button</button>

Disabled Button

A disabled button shows an action that is not available. It has lower opacity and does not react to hover or click. 

<button class="btn btn-primary" disabled>Disabled Button</button>
