Modals & Dialogs

Dialog and overlay patterns for focused user interactions. Use modals for confirmations, forms, or additional information without leaving the page.

Standard Modal

Use for confirmation dialogs, forms, or displaying additional information.

Small Modal

Use for quick confirmations or very brief information.

Large Modal

Use for complex forms, long content, or data-heavy dialogs.

Usage Guidelines

When to Use

  • Critical confirmations (deleting, irreversible actions)
  • When you need to show additional context
  • For secondary forms or interactions
  • When you need focused attention

When Not to Use

  • Main workflows should not be modal-based
  • Not ideal for large data tables
  • Avoid stacked / multiple modals
  • Be cautious with modals on mobile

Best Practices

  • Always provide a close button
  • Keep modal titles descriptive
  • Use strong action verbs on buttons
  • Ensure accessibility & focus management
  • Test on mobile for usability
