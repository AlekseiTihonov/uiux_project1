Pie & Donut Charts

Use pie and donut charts to compare parts of a whole. They work best with a small number of categories (3-5).

Pie Chart

A classic circular chart divided into sectors, illustrating numerical proportion.

Donut Chart

Ideally used when you want to include a total value or other summary info in the center.

Usage Guidelines

When to Use

  • To show proportions within a whole.
  • To compare 3–5 categories max.
  • When differences between segments are clearly visible.

Best Practices

  • Start the first slice at 12 o'clock (top).
  • Sort slices from largest to smallest clockwise.
  • Use a Donut chart if you need to display the total number.
