Pie & Donut Charts

Use pie and donut charts to compare parts of a whole. They work best with a small number of categories.

Example: Sources of Plastic Waste

Usage Guidelines

When to Use

  • To show proportions within a whole.
  • To compare 3–6 categories.
  • When differences between segments are meaningful.

When Not to Use

  • If categories are too many or too similar in size.
  • When exact values must be compared — use a bar chart instead.
  • For time-series data or trends.

Best Practices

  • Use clear, distinguishable colors for each segment.
  • Sort slices from largest to smallest when appropriate.
  • Use labels or a legend for clarity.
  • Donut charts work well when emphasizing the center text (e.g., total value).
