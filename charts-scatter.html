Scatter Plots

Use scatter plots to show the relationship between two variables.

Example: Ticket Price ($) vs. Customer Rating (1-10)

Usage Guidelines

When to Use

  • To show correlation between two numeric variables.
  • To identify outliers or clusters in the data.
  • When comparing large datasets visually.

When Not to Use

  • When you need to show trends over time — use a line chart instead.
  • For categorical data — use bar or pie charts.
  • If data points overlap too much (dense clusters).

Best Practices

  • Keep axes clearly labeled and scaled consistently.
  • Use semantic or brand colors for plot points.
  • Use tooltips or labels for more complex datasets.
  • Avoid too many points without transparency or clustering techniques.
